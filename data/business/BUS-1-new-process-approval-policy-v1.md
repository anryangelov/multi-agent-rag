# New Process Approval Policy (version 1)

This policy defines who approves a new automated process on the automation
platform before work on it starts.

## Scope

Any new workflow that will run in production, whether it is built from a
template by a business team or developed by an engineering team.

## Who approves

The **head of the department** that requests the process approves it. No
other approval is needed to start the work.

## The request

The requesting department submits a process request with:

1. the process today, step by step, and who does each step;
2. the expected benefit, in hours saved per month or errors avoided;
3. the systems the process touches;
4. the process owner: the person in the department who will be accountable
   for the automated process.

## Assessment

A process analyst from the Process Excellence Office reviews the request
within 10 working days. The analyst checks that the process is stable
enough to automate and suggests templates that fit. The assessment goes to
the department head together with the request.

## Decision

The department head approves or rejects the request in the process
portal. An approved request enters the build queue of the platform.

## Timing

The department head decides within 5 working days of receiving the
assessment. A request without a decision after 5 working days is escalated
to the head of the Process Excellence Office, who reminds the department
head once and then closes the request.

## Records

The request, the assessment and the decision are kept in the process
portal. They are the reference for later questions about why a process was
automated and who agreed to it.

## Changes after approval

If the scope of an approved process changes significantly before go-live,
the department head approves the new scope.
