# Automation Portfolio Review 2026 Q2

Prepared for the Process Board. Covers April to June 2026. Figures come
from the audit store and the business cases.

## Summary

- **21** automated processes in production at the end of June, up from 17
  at the end of March.
- **2,140** employee hours saved in the quarter, against 2,600 planned in
  the business cases.
- **2** processes paused, one of them after a data incident.
- Build backlog: 9 approved requests waiting, average wait 7 weeks.

## Top five by hours saved

| Process | Department | Hours saved in Q2 | Business case estimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Card dispute intake | Cards | 410 | 380 |
| Supplier invoice matching | Finance | 365 | 400 |
| Account closure checks | Retail Operations | 290 | 300 |
| Sanctions alert pre-screening | Compliance Operations | 240 | 350 |
| Mortgage document collection | Lending | 205 | 260 |

## Below expectations

1. **Sanctions alert pre-screening** saves 31% less than planned. The RPA
   connector to the screening system handles one session at a time and
   becomes the bottleneck at month end.
2. **Mortgage document collection** has a high manual rework rate because
   customers upload documents in unexpected formats.

## Paused processes

1. **Complaint summarisation** (Customer Care): paused on 15 March 2026
   after customer data was sent to the cloud language model through an
   experimental connector. See the incident report (CMP-8). Restart depends
   on a certified AI connector configuration and a Data Protection Office
   sign-off.
2. **Branch cash forecast** (Retail Operations): paused by the process
   owner while the branch network changes.

## Decisions requested from the board

1. Fund a second RPA robot for the screening system.
2. Make the certified AI connector mandatory for every workflow that uses
   the language model.
